The CORVET Ship Builder ×

The CORVET Ship Builder is the official web-based companion tool for CORVET, a chaotic, fast-paced skirmish tabletop game of starship combat. This application allows players to design custom spaceships and print the ship boards required for tabletop play.

About the Game

CORVET puts players directly in the corridors and engine rooms with their crew. Every turn is an exercise in crisis management: reactors fail, fires spread, and crew members are thrown off their feet by explosions. Players must make meaningful decisions about where to allocate precious power, prioritising repairs and keeping their weapons firing while the ship falls apart around them.

About the Builder Tool

In CORVET, each ship is represented by a board used for tracking crew movement, power allocation, and damage. This builder allows for the simple creation of boards for custom ships.

  • Custom Layouts: Drag and drop rooms and corridors to design your ship's physical footprint.
  • Automated Tracking: The app automatically generates doors between rooms and calculates total point costs.
  • Loadout Management: Select different Hull profiles, reactor variants and upgrades to fine-tune your ship.
  • Printer-Friendly Output: Toggle between ink-saving plain black and white boards, or beautiful printer-destroying ones.
  • Local Library: Save, load, export, and merge your ship designs entirely within your browser.

Where to get CORVET?

CORVET and the Ship Builder are currently a work in progress. While the core mechanics are functioning well, the game is in the active process of adding new content and balancing point costs.
